    How the work goes
    
Tap "Watch the foreman" at the top of the page to see the whole job done for you, with every tap shown.

    
    
      
  1. Read the copy, then tap a box in the case to take a sort. It drops into the stick at the end of the line, or just after the sort you have marked.
    2. 
      
  2. The stick shows the type as a compositor sees it: left to right, upside down. Turn on the right-reading aid if you want it the easy way round.
    3. 
      
  3. Put a 3-em space between words. The spaces are the dark, low blocks.
    4. 
      
  4. Near the end of the measure, justify the line. Step along it with the arrows, lift a space, and put in a wider or thinner one until the line is tight. Brass and copper thins take up the last point. For a short last line, quad out.
    5. 
      
  5. When a verse line is longer than the measure, set words until the next one will not go in, then tap Turn over. It spreads spaces between the words to justify the line, dumps it, and starts the runover in the stick with an em-quad indent. Finish the runover and quad it out like any short line.
    6. 
      
  6. Dump the tight line onto the galley and pull a proof. Tap "Read it against the copy" to have the proof marked the way a proofreader would. To correct a line, tap it on the galley to take it back into an empty stick. When you dump it again, it goes back in its place.
    7. 
      
  7. Distribute when you are done, because a case can run out of sorts. "Distribute by hand" brings the type back into the stick a line at a time: tap the box where each marked sort lives. Spaces go back by themselves. After two misses on one sort, its box glows. "Distribute all" throws everything back at once.
    8. 
      
  8. "Copy text" copies the galley as plain text, one line of type to a line, ready to paste into Sigla as a witness.
    9. 
      
  9. With "Case fouling possible" checked, a miss in distribution by hand is not a second try: the sort drops into the box you tapped and stays there. Later it may come out when you set from that box, looking almost right. Catch it by reading the stick (lifting it out earns a word from Tolliver) or on the proof. To clean a box, press and hold it, or tap "Look in a box," and pick out the sort that does not belong. Forgiving outlines a fouled box in red until it is clean and tells you when a stray comes out. Standard flashes the box once when the sort goes astray. Journeyman says nothing at all. Spaces go back by themselves and never foul. Unchecking the box cleans the case.
    10. 
    

    
The lay follows the California job case: lower case in the two left bays, capitals on the right with J and U after Z. There is a box for the opening quotation mark; set the closing one as two apostrophes, as compositors often did. The two thin-space boxes in the capitals' top row are an addition for convenience. A keyboard also works: letters set sorts, the space bar sets a 3-em space, Backspace lifts out, Enter dumps the line.
